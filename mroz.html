What to look for

Only surface 1 is a real yield surface. When the stress reaches it, plastic flow starts with modulus H1 and the surface is carried along. When it touches surface 2, both move together with modulus H2, and so on. The tangent modulus is E·Hm/(E+Hm), where m is the number of surfaces in contact.

On reversal the outer surfaces stay where they were left, so every stage of the reverse curve is twice as long in stress as in the monotonic curve. That is Masing's rule, and the model gives it automatically. After a small inner loop, the curve rejoins the original path exactly at the previous turning point: the model has memory.

Under cycling with a mean strain, the loops close after the first cycle and the mean stress does not relax. The translation rule has no recall term, which is why this model is not suited to ratcheting or residual stress relaxation.
