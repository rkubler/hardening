Hardening simulators

Interactive simulators of the cyclic hardening of metals, written for teaching. Each one runs entirely in the browser: change the material parameters or the loading, run, and compare the models.

Nonlinear kinematic hardening

A

Armstrong–Frederick model (1966)

One back stress with hardening and recall terms, plus Voce isotropic hardening. Monotonic test, strain cycles and stress cycles with the analytic ratcheting rate.

 B

Superposition of back stresses (Chaboche, 1986)

Up to four Armstrong–Frederick terms. See which term describes which part of the curve and how each back stress evolves.

 C

Threshold model (Chaboche, 1991)

No recall below a threshold on the last term. Compared with Chaboche for ratcheting and mean stress relaxation.

 D

Static recovery (time-dependent)

The back stress decays with time. Stress relaxation during holds, softening after a rest, and cycles with dwell.

Models designed for ratcheting

E1

Burlet–Cailletaud (1986): radial evanescence

Only the back stress component along the flow is recalled. Tension–torsion ratcheting test compared with Armstrong–Frederick, with the yield surface and plastic strain path.

 E2

Delobelle (1995)

A weighted mix of the two recall rules, tuned by δ′. Multiaxial ratcheting between the two limits.

 E3

Bari–Hassan (2000, 2002)

Three Chaboche terms with Delobelle recall and an optional threshold. Uniaxial and multiaxial ratcheting.

 E4

Ohno–Wang (1993), models I and II

Recall only near a critical state. Chaboche, Ohno–Wang I and II compared for uniaxial and multiaxial ratcheting and mean stress relaxation.

Other behaviours

M

Mróz multisurface model

Nested yield surfaces pushed by the stress in uniaxial loading: Masing behaviour, memory after inner loops, no ratcheting.

 Ma

Masing behaviour

Stabilized loops at three amplitudes and their upper branches moved to a common origin. Masing and non-Masing materials.
